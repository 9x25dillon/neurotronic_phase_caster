# Order, Absence and Records

A physical theory of water, ice and memory for the Neurotronic Phase Caster programme, stated in the language of crystallography, phase-field theory and statistical mechanics.

Estimators: `src/order_records.py` · Tests: `tests/test_order_records.py` · Experiment: `src/water_structure_assay.py`

Evidence levels: **L0** standard physics and published values; **L1** model calculations; **L2** parameters to measure; **L3** speculation.

---

## 0. Commitments

The theory adopts four axioms. Everything else is defined from them, or stated as a hypothesis with a named, measurable quantity.

- **A1 (time).** Time is the relational ordering of change. Physically, a time interval is whatever a clock measures, and a clock is a system whose state changes monotonically and irreversibly under a known law. Spacetime geometry orders events; it is the frame, and change is what gets ordered.
- **A2 (snapshot).** A configuration at time t is a section of a world-history: the projection Π_t that maps a history H to its state at t. A frozen configuration is a point of configuration space with no ongoing entropy production. It holds whatever records the history wrote into it, and nothing more.
- **A3 (record).** A record is a present property of a configuration that carries recoverable information about a past condition, written by an irreversible process and protected by an energy barrier.
- **A4 (matter).** Water and ice are symmetry-constrained, dissipative, pattern-forming systems. Their geometry is described by order parameters, symmetry groups and coarse-grained fields; their large-scale behavior follows from coarse-graining.

## 1. Primitive objects

- **Configuration space Γ.** Positions and orientations of N molecules; a microstate x ∈ Γ. A history H is a trajectory x(t). Ensembles are probability measures over histories, generated by protocols X (temperature, pressure, applied fields, the drive) through stochastic dynamics.
- **Symmetry.** The disordered phase is invariant under a group G (for the liquid, the Euclidean group E(3) plus the H₂O point symmetries). An ordered phase keeps only a subgroup H ⊂ G; for ice Ih, H is the space group P6₃/mmc on the oxygen sublattice. The degenerate ordered states form the order-parameter space G/H.
- **Crystalline order parameter (Landau–Alexander–McTague).** The Fourier amplitudes of the density, ρ_G, at reciprocal-lattice vectors G. In the liquid ⟨ρ_G⟩ = 0 at every G ≠ 0; in the crystal a set of ρ_G becomes non-zero, with relative phases fixed by the space group.
- **Local order observables o_i(x).** Examples are the tetrahedral order q_i (Errington–Debenedetti), the bond-orientational order ψ₆ (2D), Steinhardt q_l, and site occupancy.
- **Coarse-graining.** m(r, t) = Σ_i o_i W_ℓ(r − r_i) / Σ_i W_ℓ(r − r_i), with kernel width ℓ much larger than the molecular spacing. The phase field φ(r, t) is the simplest scalar m.
- **Free energy functional (Ginzburg–Landau).** F[m] = ∫ dr [ f(m) − h(r, t) m + (κ/2) |∇m|² ]. The term h is the field conjugate to m; any external drive enters here.
- **Dynamics (Hohenberg–Halperin classes).**
  - Model A (non-conserved): ∂ₜm = −Γ δF/δm + ξ.
  - Model B (conserved): ∂ₜm = ∇·(M ∇ δF/δm) + ξ.
  - Noise obeys the fluctuation–dissipation relation.
  - In general, Mori–Zwanzig projection gives ∂ₜm = −∫₀ᵗ K(t − s) δF/δm(s) ds + ξ. The kernel K encodes how long the coarse field remembers its microscopic past.

## 2. Operational definitions

### 2.1 Geometric absence

"Absence" is meaningful only relative to a reference symmetry. The theory gives it three exact senses, each with a measurement.

**(a) Reciprocal-space absence: systematic absences.** The set A_S of reciprocal-lattice points where the structure factor F(hkl) = Σ_j f_j exp(2πi(hx_j + ky_j + lz_j)) vanishes for every atomic arrangement compatible with space group S. Equivalently, these are the crystalline order-parameter components ρ_G that symmetry forces to zero.

- **Measurement.** Diffraction intensities; a reflection is absent when I(hkl) is consistent with zero at all symmetry-equivalent positions.
- **Ice Ih, oxygen sublattice (L0).** A reflection is absent iff l is odd and h − k ≡ 0 (mod 3). This combines the general P6₃/mmc conditions (000l and hh2̄hl with l odd, from the 6₃ screw axis and the c-glide) with the Wyckoff 4f special condition.
- **Code.** `systematic_absences` finds every zero that survives all values of the free coordinate z: 68 reflections with indices up to 3, all matching the rule. F(0001) = 0 to machine precision while F(0002) = 4 cos(4πz).
- **Reading.** These absences carry information: they identify the screw axes and glide planes.

**(b) Real-space absence: the order-deficit field and its singular set.** For a reference order magnitude m₀, the order deficit is a(r) = 1 − |m(r)|/m₀ ∈ [0, 1]. The defect set D is where |m| → 0 at the coarse-graining scale. Defects are classified by the homotopy groups of G/H and carry topological charges, which are winding numbers s = (1/2π)∮∇θ·dl for a phase θ.

- **Examples in ice:**
  - vacancies and interstitials;
  - Bjerrum L and D defects (a hydrogen bond carrying 0 or 2 protons, which breaks the ice rules);
  - ionic defects (H₃O⁺, OH⁻);
  - dislocations, grain boundaries, and Ih/Ic stacking faults.
- **Measurement.** Defect densities from dielectric relaxation and DC conductivity (Bjerrum and ionic defects), positron annihilation (vacancies), X-ray diffuse and topography methods (dislocations), and diffraction line shapes (stacking disorder). In simulation, local-order classification.
- **Code.** `hexatic_defects` identifies sites with coordination z ≠ 6 in a 2D crystal and assigns charge 6 − z. A vacancy appears as a cluster of three defect sites with net charge 0: one missing site carries no net disclination charge.

**(c) Absence of long-range order: the liquid.** Liquid water is the phase in which G is unbroken: ⟨ρ_G⟩ = 0 at long range. Local tetrahedral order persists with a finite correlation length ξ and a finite lifetime. Each molecule forms close to four hydrogen bonds with about 1 ps lifetime in a nearly tetrahedral configuration (L0).

- **Measurement.** The radial distribution g(r) and structure factor S(k) give ξ. The distribution of q (`tetrahedral_order`: exactly 1 in ideal ice, 0.009 for an ideal gas in the test) and two-state analyses (the fraction s of locally favored tetrahedral structures) give the local order.

In this theory, then, water has local tetrahedral geometry without long-range order. The word "absence" refers to (a), (b) or (c), and to nothing else.

### 2.2 Morphic field

**Definition.** The morphic field of a sample is its coarse-grained order-parameter field m(r, t) together with the constitutive law that evolves it: F[m], the dynamical class (A or B), and the memory kernel K. "Form" means morphology: the level sets of m, its defect set D, and its structure factor S(k, t).

**Morphic memory (L0, measurable).** The history dependence of form. Let X be a past protocol variable, chosen at random, and R(t) a coarse observable of m at a later time t. Morphic memory is the record fidelity I(X; R(t)) from §2.3, together with its decay time τ_M.

- **Established mechanisms:** hysteresis, aging, defect populations, residual stress, and polymorph selection by surviving seeds. Each is mediated by physical degrees of freedom inside the sample.
- **Bounds.** τ_M is bounded below by the decay of K, and above by the escape times of the metastable states that hold the information (§2.3).

**Non-local component (L3 hypothesis, defined so it can be measured).** For two samples A and B, define

μ_AB = the conditional mutual information between Form_A and History_B, given C_AB and History_A

in bits, where C_AB is every physical channel between the samples: shared air and dust, seed crystals, electromagnetic fields, vibration, temperature, and the experimenter. Standard physics predicts μ_AB = 0. The morphic-resonance hypothesis predicts μ_AB > 0.

- **Estimation.** Decode History_B (randomized, blinded) from Form_A, with samples separated, sealed, and filtered against seeds. This is the two-cell extension of `water_structure_assay`.
- **Classic confound.** The historical "disappearing polymorph" cases, where a new crystal form takes over laboratories worldwide, are explained by microscopic seed contamination (Dunitz and Bernstein, 1995). C_AB must exclude seeds by design.

### 2.3 Mathematical impression (record)

**Definition.** A record of a past variable X in a present configuration Y = Π_t(H) is a carrier observable R = g(Y) together with a readout map X̂ = d(R). It is characterized by four numbers.

- **Fidelity:** I(X; R) in bits, with a permutation p-value and correction for estimator bias. Code: `record_fidelity`.
- **Lifetime:** τ_R, from I(X; R(t)) ≈ I₀ exp(−t/τ_R). Code: `record_lifetime`.
- **Stability condition:** the carrier sits in a metastable basin with barrier ΔF, giving τ_R ≈ τ₀ exp(ΔF/kT) (Kramers). One hour at 273 K needs ΔF ≈ 36 kT. Code: `kramers_lifetime`, `barrier_for_lifetime`. Without a barrier, a correlation relaxes at the microscopic rate and never becomes a record. This is why liquid water, with hydrogen bonds lasting about 1 ps, cannot hold a structural record in its bond network.
- **Cost and direction:** a record is written by an irreversible process, and resetting the carrier for reuse dissipates at least kT ln 2 per bit (Landauer). Code: `landauer_cost_j`. Because writing produces entropy, records point to the past: a variable chosen after the snapshot has zero fidelity. This is the retro test in the assay.

**Fractal scaling as one record format (L0 criteria).** A morphology is *approximately fractal* only when all three hold:

1. The box-counting fit spans at least one decade of scale.
2. Every local slope lies within a stated tolerance of the fitted dimension D.
3. D is non-integer.

Code: `box_counting_dimension`. The Sierpinski carpet passes with D = 1.893 = ln 8 / ln 3. Coarsening interfaces fail: their local slope is 1.0 below the domain size and crosses toward 2 above it.

Real dendrites, snow crystals and DLA clusters (D ≈ 1.71 in 2D) show self-similarity over limited windows, and the window must be reported with D.

### 2.4 Time-dependent geometry: the geometric clock

**Definition.** A geometric clock is a dynamical law under which a configuration's length scale grows monotonically with the time since a reference event, together with a calibrated inverse map from length to time.

**Phase ordering (L0).** After a quench, dynamical scaling holds: S(k, t) = L(t)^d g(kL(t)), with L ∝ t^(1/z).

- z = 2 for non-conserved order (model A, Allen–Cahn).
- z = 3 for conserved order (model B, Lifshitz–Slyozov–Wagner and Cahn–Hilliard).

A frozen snapshot therefore records two things:
- **Elapsed time**, as t̂ = L⁻¹(L_snapshot), with an ensemble interval.
- **The conservation law**, as the exponent.

**Measured here (L1).**

- **Effective exponents:** 0.42 ± 0.01 for model A on 256² and 0.333 ± 0.005 for model B on 128². Finite windows sit below the asymptotic 1/2.
- **Example readings:**
  - model A: a snapshot at t = 100 reads as 98 (2σ range 79–140);
  - model B: a snapshot at t = 800 reads as 840 (705–1048).
- **Precision:** δt/t ≈ (1/β)(δL/L), where β = 1/z.
- **Domain count:** precision falls when the box holds too few domains. Below about 25 domains, the clock marks its reading unreliable (`ClockReading.reliable`); 3σ outliers were observed at 13 to 16 domains.

The length L is measured from the interface density. The first moment of S(k) is biased in 2D because Porod's k^-(d+1) tail makes it diverge logarithmically.

This is the literal sense in which a configuration is an imprint of time's passage: time is inferred from geometry through a dynamical law, with error bars.

### 2.5 Projection ("shadow")

A snapshot is Π_t(H). The information a snapshot retains about its history is I(H; Π_t(H)). Records are exactly the part of that information which survives coarse-graining and thermal noise. "Shadow" in this theory means Π_t and nothing more: a section of a history, carrying records of the change that produced it.

## 3. Water and ice in this language

- **Liquid water.** Unbroken G, local tetrahedral order, hydrogen-bond lifetime about 1 ps. Collective intermolecular modes appear near 1.8 THz (hydrogen-bond bending) and 5.7 THz (stretching). THz absorption rises with temperature. Structural records in the bond network last picoseconds (L0); longer memory in a water sample needs a carrier with a barrier: heat (seconds), dissolved gas or solutes (diffusion times), interfaces and impurities.
- **Ice Ih: oxygen order.** Long-range order with oxygens in P6₃/mmc.
- **Ice Ih: proton disorder.** Hydrogens are disordered under the Bernal–Fowler ice rules. Proton disorder is frozen in, leaving the Pauling residual entropy S₀ = R ln(3/2) = 3.37 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ (calorimetry gives about 3.4). This is a record of non-equilibrium freezing: the proton-ordered equilibrium phase, ice XI, forms only below about 72 K and only with dopants that supply mobile defects. Proton reordering proceeds through Bjerrum and ionic defects.
- **Stacking disorder.** Ice grown from supercooled water or vapor at low temperature contains mixed cubic and hexagonal stacking ("stacking-disordered ice"). The stacking sequence records the formation conditions.
- **Snow crystals.** Habit alternates with temperature (plates, columns, plates, columns across roughly −4, −10 and −22 °C), and branching grows with supersaturation (the Nakaya diagram). A crystal is a record of its temperature and supersaturation path, readable shell by shell.
- **Ice cores.** Isotope ratios (δ¹⁸O) and annual layers record temperature over hundreds of thousands of years. This is a calibrated physical record with a dating chain, the strongest example of ice recording the passage of time.

## 4. Vocabulary translation

- "Water is a crystal" → liquid water has local tetrahedral order (measured by q and by the locally-favored-structure fraction) with finite correlation length and ~1 ps lifetimes. Ice Ih has long-range order with space group P6₃/mmc.
- "Geometric absence" → (a) systematic absences, the symmetry-forced zeros of ρ_G; (b) the order-deficit field a(r) and the defect set D with its topological charges; (c) unbroken symmetry in the liquid.
- "Morphic field" → the coarse-grained order field m(r, t) with F[m], its dynamical class, and its memory kernel K. Its non-local extension is the measurable μ_AB.
- "Mathematical impression" → a record (carrier, readout, fidelity, lifetime, barrier, cost).
- "Ice as time-dependent geometry" → phase ordering and growth under dynamical scaling: the geometric clock.
- "Shadow" → the projection Π_t of a history onto a configuration.
- "Fractal impression" → a morphology with box-counting dimension D over a reported scaling window, meeting the criteria in §2.3.
- "Informational frequency node" → a drive frequency ω coupled through the dielectric loss ε''(ω). At about 1 THz it couples to the hydrogen-bond network's relaxation and collective modes. In F it appears through the conjugate field h and through absorbed power ω ε₀ ε''(ω) ⟨E²⟩.
- "Geometric layline" → the spatial profile of the drive across the array. A helical phase of charge ℓ produces a field with a vortex (an on-axis null) and a ring of intensity.
- "Stream of consciousness written into water" → a protocol variable X(t) whose record is sought in R. The assay estimates I(X; R) against controls.
- "Communication across temporal dimensions" → the fidelity function I(X_t ; R_{t+Δ}) over offsets Δ. Positive Δ measures memory. Negative Δ must give zero and serves as an integrity check.

## 5. The Phase Caster drive in this theory

The THz field oscillates about 10¹² times per second. Any coarse structural order m at scale ℓ relaxes far more slowly than one cycle, so m responds to cycle averages: absorbed power (heating) and other terms in ⟨E²⟩. A drive can therefore write into m only through the spatial pattern of intensity I(r); the instantaneous phase averages out.

Imprinting a winding number directly into an order parameter requires an order parameter that is itself a coherent complex field locked to the drive's phase. Superfluids, Bose–Einstein condensates and driven spin systems qualify (L0). The hydrogen-bond network of water at 300 K has no such macroscopic phase (L0).

The layline's charge ℓ thus reaches the water as the geometry of a heating pattern: a central spot for ℓ = 0, a ring for ℓ ≠ 0. That geometry relaxes by thermal diffusion. This yields prediction P2 below, which the assay tests.

## 6. Falsifiable predictions

- **P1 (liquid persistence).** In liquid water, I(X; R) falls to zero within the classical memory horizon set by heat transport (219 s for the assay cell). Test: assay H1 and H2. A positive beyond the horizon, absent in sham and heater-matched arms, falsifies P1.
- **P2 (layline).** A layline's charge is recoverable only while the heating pattern persists. With equal absorbed power and a heater control matched in spatial pattern, charge decoding is at chance at every time. Test: assay codebook equalization plus a patterned heater arm.
- **P3 (crystals write records).** If symbols are written as interface heating patterns during crystal growth, they become decodable from the dopant striation profile. Interface temperature sets the growth rate, and the growth rate sets dopant uptake (Burton–Prim–Slichter). The records last until solid-state diffusion erases them, which takes geological time at room temperature and days near the melting point. Standard physics predicts success, and growth striations already record history in natural and grown crystals. Implemented in `src/crystal_writer.py` ([CRYSTAL_WRITER.md](CRYSTAL_WRITER.md)):
  - Ge:Ga by Czochralski with 8 mK Peltier pulses, means over 48 runs: 65 % symbol accuracy with 16 symbols (4 levels × ring charges ℓ = 0–3, chance 6.25 %), and 98.5 % with 4 symbols (2 levels × spot/ring). With Hamming(7,4), 92 % of 4-symbol messages read back exactly. These replace an earlier single-run figure of 86 %.
  - KDP from solution with 1550 nm pump heating carries the spot/ring code at 77 % (75 % with clock-track referencing).
  - Lateral heat spreading at the interface washes out the fine ring charges ℓ = 1, 2, 3, while spot against ring survives. The crystal keeps layline geometry at the coarse level only.
  - Under a ±10 % growth-rate ramp, referencing each band to the clock track keeps the Ge:Ga 4-symbol mode at 98 % (96 % of messages exact). Without the reference it falls to 69 %.
  - The THz photomixer array alone is too weak to write.
  The same scheme applies to ice: directional freezing with grain, stacking or solute records.
- **P4 (geometric clock).** Snapshot domain size gives elapsed time since a quench with precision (1/β)(δL/L), and its exponent identifies the conservation law. Test: `CoarseningClock`; experimentally, phase-separating or grain-coarsening samples imaged at known times.
- **P5 (non-local form).** μ_AB = 0 within the bound the two-cell protocol can set. A significant μ_AB, under seed exclusion and blinding, falsifies P5.
- **P6 (arrow).** The fidelity for variables chosen after the snapshot is zero. Test: the assay's retro window and negative lags.

## 7. What the theory leaves out, and why

The theory makes no claim that water is a geometric absence, that crystallography is a morphic field, or that time emerges from fractal impressions. Each can enter only as a hypothesis with a named quantity (μ_AB for non-local form; a decodable record beyond known carriers for persistent water structure) and an experiment that can return zero.

High-dilution "memory of water" claims did not survive blinded replication (Nature's 1988 investigation of the Benveniste laboratory). That history is why the assay is blinded, preregistered and hash-committed.

## 9. Evidence levels

- **L0.** Space-group absences; ice rules and the Pauling entropy; the tetrahedral order definition; Ginzburg–Landau free energy and model A/B dynamics; dynamical scaling and the t^(1/2) and t^(1/3) laws; Kramers escape; Landauer's bound; topological defect classification; mutual information.
- **L1.** Numerical phase-field results, clock precision, the reliability threshold, and the application of these laws to the drive.
- **L2.** Material and device parameters (defect densities, kernel widths, barrier heights) in any specific sample.
- **L3.** μ_AB > 0, persistent information-bearing structure in liquid water beyond known carriers, and time emerging from fractal structure. Each is stated as a hypothesis with an experiment that can return zero.
